Support my work on OurCat

OurCat started with my own cat, Tokyo. I’m working on making everyday logging, reminders and shared care easier for everyone who helps look after a cat.

OurCat keeps everyone’s entries in sync, so when your partner or sitter logs something it shows up on your phone too. That means storing the records securely in the cloud, and that costs money every month just to keep the app working, before anything new gets built.
