TESTING FEEDBACK

Tell us what you noticed.

A short message is fine. We want to hear what helped, what was confusing and what didn't work as expected.

Use the short form

Eight quick questions. Only three need an answer.

Open the feedback form

Or send an email

Write in your own words. Mention your phone and Android version if something didn't work.

Email your feedback

What to include

  • What you were trying to do.
  • What you expected, and what happened instead.
  • Your phone and Android version, if something didn't work.
  • For ideas or things you liked, what made them useful.

Please leave out passwords, invitation codes, medication instructions, private notes, other people's details and screenshots.

Other ways to reach us

Google Play may also let you send private feedback from OurCat's page in the Play Store. For help joining the test, email mycatapps@gmail.com. To delete your account, use the account deletion page instead.

What happens next

We read everything, group similar reports and may ask a follow-up question if you said we could. We can't promise to act on every suggestion or reply to every message. Form answers are kept privately in the OurCat Google account; see the privacy policy.
