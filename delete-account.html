ACCOUNT & DATA

Request account deletion.

You can request deletion of your OurCat account without opening or reinstalling the app.

Email an account-deletion request

Or email mycatapps@gmail.com with the subject “OurCat account deletion request”.

Verify that the account is yours

Send the request from the email address registered with OurCat. We confirm ownership through a reply and confirmation from that address. If you cannot access it, explain that to support so an appropriate verification route can be agreed.

Do not send passwords, sign-in codes, invitation codes or private care information.

What happens to the information

After verification and the applicable shared-care process, eligible account records, the authentication identity and personal identity links are removed or pseudonymised. Solely owned cat/household records and associated uploads can be removed where the supported deletion conditions are met.

For a co-parent, shared cat information and care history needed by the remaining authorised carers can survive, with personal identity links cleaned.

Shared owners

If you own a cat shared with another carer, the request can enter a server-controlled 14-day survival window. Shared household information is preserved during that window. You can reclaim the account during that window. A request can expire, requiring shared ownership to be resolved before a fresh request is submitted. We will explain any steps needed to complete the request; contact support if you cannot use the app.

Timing and follow-up

We will verify and process requests without unnecessary delay, and explain any applicable shared-care or required retention exception.

Questions about handling information? Read the privacy-policy draft or contact support.
