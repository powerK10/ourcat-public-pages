FOR INVITED TESTERS

You're invited to try OurCat.

Joining takes about five minutes. You'll use two separate accounts: your Google account to install the app from Google Play, and an OurCat account that you create inside the app.

You need an invitation first. The test is private, so OurCat isn't in ordinary Google Play search. Your invitation email contains the joining link. If you haven't got one yet, request an invitation.

Join in four steps

  1. Use the right Google account. On your phone, make sure Google Play is signed in with the email address you gave us for the test.
  2. Open the joining link from your invitation and tap Become a tester.
  3. Install OurCat from the Google Play link shown after you join. It can take a little while to appear.
  4. Create your OurCat account with an email address and password, confirm your email, then add a made-up cat and record one food or water entry.

What should I try first?

Start small: record one thing, then find it on Today and in History. If someone else is testing with you, invite them to your made-up cat and compare what you each see.

Having trouble?

Google Play says I'm not a tester

Check that Google Play is signed in with the email address we added to the test. If it still doesn't work, reply to your invitation email. Please don't post your email address in a public group.

I can't find OurCat in Google Play

Private tests don't show up in search. Use the joining link in your invitation, then the install link that appears after you join.

I joined, but I can't install it yet

Google Play can take some time to make the app available after you join. Try the link again later, and tell us what Google Play shows if it still doesn't work.

Do I need to enter my real cat's details?

No. A made-up cat is enough for everything we ask you to try.

How do I stop taking part?

Tell us and we'll stop sending test messages. You can also leave the test from the joining link. Leaving the test doesn't delete your OurCat account: for that, see account deletion.

Need a hand? Email mycatapps@gmail.com and describe what you see. Never send passwords.
