For invited testers

Let’s get you started

Use the invitation for your device: Google Play on Android, or Apple’s TestFlight on iPhone/iPad. I’ll arrange your invitation through mycatapps@gmail.com.

Waiting for an invitation? Request one here. The test is private. Android and iPhone/iPad invitations are available.

Android · Google Play

  1. Check your Google Play email

    Open the Play Store and tap your profile picture. Make sure it shows the email you gave me. Use that same account when you open the invitation link.

  2. Open your invitation link

    Tap Become a tester on the Google Play page.

  3. Install OurCat

    Follow the Google Play installation link shown after you join. Availability can take a little while to update.

  4. Create an OurCat account

    Open the app, sign up with an email and password, and confirm your email. This is separate from the Google account used to install it.

One favour for Android testers: please stay in the test for 14 days in a row. You don’t have to open the app every day. Google needs a group of testers to stay in that long before an app can go public, so staying opted in helps me get OurCat ready for release.

iPhone or iPad · TestFlight

These steps are for when your TestFlight invitation arrives by email.

  1. Install TestFlight

    Get Apple’s TestFlight app on your iPhone or iPad. It currently needs iOS or iPadOS 16 or later.

  2. Accept your invitation

    Open Apple’s invitation email on that device, accept it and choose Install for OurCat. There’s no Google Play account or 14-day opt-in requirement for iPhone/iPad.

  3. Create an OurCat account

    Open OurCat, sign up with an email and password, and confirm your email. This is separate from accepting the TestFlight invitation.

TestFlight builds expire after up to 90 days; TestFlight shows the remaining time. If your build expires or no build is available, reply to me for help.

Start with one small thing

Add your cat, or a made-up one, record food or water, then find the entry on Today and in History. That’s enough to get started.

Testing with your partner or a cat-sitter? Once you’re both in, invite them from the app and check you can both see the same entry.

Something not working?

Google Play says the app isn’t available to me

Check the Google account in both your browser and the Play Store. It must match the one in your invitation. Also check your Play country is eligible. If those match, reply to the invitation with the message you see.

I joined on Google Play, but can’t install yet

Google Play can take several hours to make a test or update available. Try your invitation link again later. If it still doesn’t work, reply to the invitation email.

I can’t find the app in search

On Android, use the joining link in your invitation, then the installation link Google Play shows. On iPhone/iPad, use your TestFlight invitation. This is not a public App Store release.

I can’t find my confirmation email

Look for the confirmation email sent after you signed up. Check your spam folder and the address shown in the app. If it hasn’t arrived, choose Send another link when the countdown ends, or correct the address if it is wrong. After opening the link, return to OurCat; tap Check again if you’re still waiting. If that doesn’t help, email mycatapps@gmail.com. Never send your password or confirmation link.

How do I stop taking part?

You can leave at any time. On Android, open the joining link and leave the Google Play test. On iPhone/iPad, open OurCat in TestFlight and choose Stop Testing. Email me if you’d like test messages to stop. Leaving the test or uninstalling doesn’t delete your OurCat account; request account deletion separately.
