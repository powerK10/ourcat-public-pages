How OurCat started

Hi, I make OurCat. The black-and-white one in the photos is Tokyo.

Tokyo is the first pet my partner and I have had together, and we were anxious about getting everything right. We wrote it all down, but our notes were spread across two phones and old messages, so we still kept asking each other who did what and when.

Then Tokyo had an accident. I needed the vet’s number and the emergency vet’s details straight away, and I didn’t have them to hand.

I started making OurCat to keep everything in one place, easy to find and in sync between us, even if one of us lost or changed a phone. Friends who cat-sit can use it too.

It’s still early days, which is why I’d love your help.

Where it fits in a normal week

  1. Monday, 08:05

    Your partner feeds the cat before work and logs it. You see it on your phone and don’t feed the cat twice.

  2. Friday, away for the weekend

    A friend’s cat-sitting. You give them three days’ access, so they can see the routine and log what they do. When the three days are up, their access ends by itself.

It works fine on your own, too. Sharing is there for when someone else helps out.
