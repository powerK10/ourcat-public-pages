How early access works

Record your cat’s care and see what your partner or cat sitter has done. Use your own cat or a made-up one, whenever it suits you.

  • Free, and stays free for you
  • Android or iPhone/iPad
  • Ages 16+, in the UK, US, Canada or Australia
  • I reply within a day

Your Google Play or App Store country must be one of those four. On iPhone and iPad you’ll use TestFlight, Apple’s free app for trying early versions of apps before they’re public. It needs iOS or iPadOS 16 or later.

What happens next

  1. Send a request

    Tell me which device you’d like to use, your invitation email, country and age. For Android, use your Google Play email. Testing both? Give each store’s country in the form.

    Testing with a partner or cat sitter? They need their own compatible device and their own request. Mention each other in the form’s optional question and I’ll invite you together.

  2. Hear back within a day

    I’ll email to say I’ve got your request. For Android, I’ll add your Google Play email to the private list and send joining details. For iPhone/iPad, I’ll send a TestFlight invitation. Sending the form doesn’t install anything or enrol you yet.

  3. Try it when it suits you

    Add your cat, or a made-up one, and record something. Send feedback whenever you have something to share.

How much is involved?

If you can, stay opted in to the Google Play test for 14 days in a row and try the app during that time. You don’t need to open it every day or leave a positive review.

A few thoughts in your own words are enough. The optional things to try cover reminders and shared care.

Testing is free, voluntary and unpaid. Joining doesn’t start a subscription, and you can stop at any time.

Questions people ask

Can I use my real cat?

Yes, it’s up to you. Your own cat or a made-up one both work. I’ll do my best to keep your records, and you’ll carry on using the app as normal once it’s public.

How long does early access last?

There’s no fixed end date. If anything about your access is going to change, I’ll email you first.

Will it stay free for me?

Yes. Everything in OurCat is open to you now, and it stays free for you.

Can I test with someone I share care with?

Yes, please. Sharing is the bit I most want to see working in real life. Each of you needs your own eligible device and invitation. Android uses Google Play; iPhone/iPad uses TestFlight. Once you’re both in, the cat’s owner invites the other person from the app with an invite code.

I have an iPhone. Can I take part?

Yes. Request an invitation using the same form and I’ll reply within a day with an invitation to TestFlight, Apple’s free app for trying early versions of apps. You don’t need a Google Play account for iPhone/iPad.

Can I test if I don’t have a cat?

Yes. A made-up cat works for everything I ask.

What if I’m outside the eligible countries or need help?

Email mycatapps@gmail.com. I can help with questions, but can’t promise access outside the current test countries.
