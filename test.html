EARLY ACCESS · ANDROID

Help make everyday cat care a little clearer.

Has someone already fed the cat? What's due next? OurCat helps the people caring for the same cats keep those answers in one place.

We're inviting cat carers to try an early version on Android and tell us what makes sense, what's confusing and what would genuinely help.

What can I do in OurCat?

Record today's care

Log food, water, litter, medication and other care as it happens.

See what's due

Set care reminders for each cat and check what still needs doing today.

Share the care

Invite a co-parent, or a temporary co-parent whose access ends automatically.

Look back

Check History before the next decision, and keep useful details in Care info.

How testing works

  1. Request an invitation. Tell us a little about your phone and how you care for cats.
  2. Get your joining instructions. If there's a place for you, we add your Google Play email to the private tester list and send you a link to join and install.
  3. Try a few things. Start with a made-up cat, record some care and, if it suits you, try reminders or sharing with another person.
  4. Tell us what you think. A sentence or two is enough, through the feedback page or by email.

Please stay opted in to the Google Play test for at least 14 days in a row. You don't have to open OurCat every day or leave a review.

Who can take part?

Anyone aged 16 or over whose Google Play country is the UK, United States, Canada or Australia, with an Android phone. You might care for one cat or several, on your own or with other people. No technical knowledge is needed.

Taking part is voluntary and unpaid. We may have fewer places than people who ask.

Do I need to use real information?

No. A made-up cat and made-up care records are enough. Please don't enter real medication details, microchip numbers or other people's contact details just for the test.

Early access has no fixed end date. We intend your account and records to carry over when OurCat becomes publicly available, but we can't promise a date or uninterrupted service. We'll tell you before anything about your access changes, and you can ask for your account to be deleted at any time.

OurCat is a record-keeping tool. It doesn't watch your cat or give veterinary advice, so keep your usual care arrangements and reminders for anything urgent.

How do sharing and alerts work?

The cat's owner decides who can see and record its care. Owners and co-parents get phone alerts for reminders and when someone else in the household records care.

A temporary co-parent's access ends at the time the owner chose, and the owner can end it sooner. In the current test version, temporary co-parents see their reminders inside OurCat rather than as phone alerts.

Before you ask to join

We use what you tell us only to decide whether the test suits you and to contact you about it. Your answers are kept privately in the OurCat Google account. Please don't send passwords or private care details. To withdraw your request, or have your details deleted, email mycatapps@gmail.com.
