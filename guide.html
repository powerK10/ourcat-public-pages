Try what interests you

A few minutes with the app can be useful. You can skip these suggestions and simply tell me what you noticed.

Use a made-up cat and demo records. Keep your usual reminders for medication and anything urgent.

A first look

  1. Add a made-up cat

    Miso is a good name to borrow. Skip any optional details you don’t want to fill in.

  2. Record something

    Add a food or water entry. Is it clear what to enter and which cat it’s for?

  3. Find it again

    Look on Today and in History. Can you see what happened and when?

If you’d like to explore more

Try a reminder
Set one for your made-up cat. Notice whether the alert arrives and what happens when you open it. Phone alerts depend on your role, the test version and Android settings.
Share with your partner, a cat-sitter or another invited tester
Once they’re in the test too, invite them to your demo household. Record some care and compare what each of you sees. Keep invitation codes private.
Add another cat
Switch between two made-up cats. Is it clear which one you’re recording care for?

What would help me?

Tell me what you tried, what worked, or where you hesitated. Something that felt unnecessary is useful feedback too.
