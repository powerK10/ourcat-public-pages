Things to try, if you fancy it

Even five minutes helps. Start with one care entry, and come back to the rest when you have more time.

Use your own cat or a made-up one, whichever you prefer. Keep your usual reminders for medication and anything urgent.

A first look

  1. Add a cat

    Your own, or a made-up one (Miso is a good name to borrow). Skip any details you’d rather not fill in.

  2. Record something

    Add a food or water entry. Is it clear what to enter and which cat it’s for?

  3. Find it again

    Look on Today and in History. Can you see what happened and when?

If you’d like to explore more

Try a reminder
Set one for your cat. Notice whether the alert arrives and what happens when you open it. Phone alerts depend on your role, the test version and Android settings.
Share with your partner, a cat-sitter or another invited tester
Once they’re in the test too, invite them from the app. Record some care and compare what each of you sees. Keep invitation codes private.
Add another cat
Add a second cat, real or made up, and switch between them. Is it clear which one you’re recording care for?

What I’d love to hear

What you tried, what worked, and where you got stuck or hesitated. If something felt pointless, tell me that too.
