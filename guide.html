TESTER GUIDE

Small things to try. Honest feedback.

You don't need to test every screen or write a technical report. Try what matters for how you care for a cat, and tell us what happened.

Start with a made-up cat. Use made-up names and records. Don't rely on OurCat as your only reminder for medication or anything else urgent.

Your first ten minutes

  1. Create your OurCat account with an email address and password, and confirm your email.
  2. Add a made-up cat, for example Miso. You can skip the optional details.
  3. Record a food or water entry.
  4. Find it on Today and in History. Can you tell when it happened?
  5. Tell us if anything felt confusing or didn't work.

If you're taking part in a guided session with us, follow the session instructions instead of this guide.

Over the next two weeks

Everyday care

Record a few different kinds of care. Is it easy to find them again later?

Reminders

Set up a reminder for your made-up cat. Note whether an alert arrives and what happens when you tap it.

Sharing

If another tester is with you, invite them to your made-up cat and check what each of you can see.

Two cats

Add a second made-up cat. Is it always clear which cat you're recording for?

Explore freely too. The moments when you hesitate, or decide not to use something, are as useful to us as the things that work.

What feedback helps?

One or two sentences: what you tried, what you expected and what happened. Tell us too when something was useful, confusing or unnecessary.

Good to know

How often should I open the app?

Whenever it suits you. We only ask that you stay opted in to the Google Play test for at least 14 days in a row. You don't need to open OurCat every day.

Can I use my real cat?

You can, but a made-up cat is enough for testing. Early access has no fixed end date, and we intend accounts and records to carry over when OurCat becomes publicly available, but we can't guarantee it. Keep your usual reminders for anything important.

Who gets phone alerts?

Owners and co-parents get phone alerts for reminders and when someone else records care. In the current test version, temporary co-parents see their reminders inside OurCat rather than as phone alerts. Alerts also depend on your Android notification settings.

Which version do I have?

Open OurCat in Google Play and look under About this app. Mention the version in your feedback if you can.

Can I delete my account?

Yes. Go to Settings → Account & privacy in the app, or use the account deletion page.
