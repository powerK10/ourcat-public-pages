A little help with OurCat.

Tell us what happened and we will help you find the next step.

Include the app version, phone model and a short description. Redact any screenshots before sending them.

Keep private details private

Do not send passwords, sign-in codes, invitation codes, medical notes or another person’s information. We do not need private cat-care details to answer an ordinary support question.

Support OurCat’s development

OurCat is free to use. If you would like to support its development, you can make an optional contribution through our Revolut payment page. A contribution does not unlock features or change your access, and Revolut handles the payment.

Account deletion

You can request deletion without opening or reinstalling OurCat.

Health concerns

For concerns about a cat’s health, contact a vet. OurCat support is not an emergency or veterinary service.
