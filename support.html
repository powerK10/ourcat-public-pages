Support development

OurCat keeps everyone’s entries in sync, so when your partner or sitter logs something it shows up on your phone too. That means storing the records securely in the cloud, and that costs money every month just to keep the app working, before anything new gets built. If you’d like to help with that, you can contribute through Revolut.
