PRIVACY POLICY

Your information in OurCat.

OurCat is published under the developer name mycatapps. Contact mycatapps@gmail.com with privacy questions.

Last updated: 15 October 2026. This policy describes the private Android closed test of OurCat, intended for people aged 16 and over.

What you provide

Your email address and password to create an account. Cat profiles and practical information, such as the cat's name, care notes, vet details, microchip number and optional pet-insurance provider and policy reference. Care records, reminders and preferences. Optional photos. Your optional display name and telephone number, and the names, relationships, phone numbers or email addresses you choose to enter for vets, carers or emergency contacts. OurCat does not read your phone's address book.

Care notes and other free-text fields can contain personal information if you type it, including information about other people. Enter only information you are comfortable sharing with the authorised carers of that cat.

Outdoor records can include a location description that you type about your cat. OurCat does not use your phone's location for that feature, and the description is not an automatic report of where your phone is. The Android closed test offers email and password sign-in only. Google sign-in is not available.

Information used to run the service

The app generates identifiers, timestamps, the cat's home time zone, membership and access state, reminder completions, upload details and deletion-request status.

OurCat uses Google Firebase to sign you in, store and synchronise records and photos, run protected backend features and deliver notifications. To do this, Firebase processes your account identifier and email address, and also:

  • a Firebase installation identifier and a notification-delivery token;
  • IP addresses;
  • app version and device and software information;
  • the name of the backend feature the app asks it to run.

Firebase creates the installation identifier and delivery token automatically when the app starts. Refusing notification permission stops notifications from being shown, but it does not stop these identifiers being created.

Limited account-scoped information can be stored on your phone, including unfinished care updates and reminder preferences. A care update saved on the phone while you are offline is sent to the cloud when connectivity returns.

Google's backend services monitor Cloud Function execution counts, status, execution time and memory use at project and function level. This supports backend operation and troubleshooting. It is separate from Firebase Analytics and crash reporting.

Why we use it

To create and secure accounts, keep care records, show the right cat, share care with the carers you authorise, deliver reminders and care notifications, store optional photos, enforce access and limits, answer support requests and process deletion requests.

We do not use your information for advertising. We do not sell it. We do not use Firebase Analytics or an advertising SDK in this release. Normal service operations still process the technical information described below.

Who can see it

Carers you authorise can see the relevant cat's information according to their household role and access. Some information and actions are available only to the cat's owner. Temporary carers have time-limited access.

If you share an invitation code, or export History as a CSV file and share it, the app or person you choose receives that content and handles it under their own rules.

Google provides OurCat's sign-in, database, file storage, backend functions and notification delivery through Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage for Firebase, Cloud Functions and Firebase Cloud Messaging. Google processes OurCat's records to provide these services for us.

Google also handles some technical Service Data to provide, secure, maintain and improve its Firebase services, and to meet legal obligations. Our project's optional setting that would let Google use this Service Data for non-Firebase Google services is switched off. Google's own Firebase processing continues. See Firebase privacy information and the Google Cloud Privacy Notice.

Support emails are handled through our public support mailbox.

Where information is processed

Our cat-record database and photo storage are located in the United States. Firebase Authentication runs from US data centres. Other Firebase processing, support and technical logs can take place in other countries. Google's terms describe the safeguards it applies to international transfers.

Crash reporting and analytics

Crash reporting and its supporting session-measurement component are configured off for this closed test. Firebase Analytics is not used. Any later optional crash-reporting programme would need a separate explanation and your individual choice before any collection starts.

Security

Information sent between the app and Firebase uses encrypted HTTPS connections. This protects information while it travels. It is not end-to-end encryption: authorised servers process the records so that sign-in and shared care work.

Account access is authenticated, and shared information is restricted by role and access controls. Before upload, the app removes embedded metadata, including camera location data, from photos in supported formats (JPEG, PNG and WebP). Anything visible in the picture itself can still be seen by authorised carers.

Use a secure password, and share invitations only with the carers you intend.

Keeping and deleting information

We keep information only while it is needed for your account, shared care and the necessary operation of the service. We do not keep information simply because it might be useful one day.

You can request account deletion in Settings → Account & privacy, or request it here without installing the app. After the request is verified and the applicable shared-care step is complete:

  • your account records, sign-in identity and personal identity links are removed or pseudonymised;
  • cats and households that only you own, and their uploads, can be removed where the supported deletion conditions are met.

If you are a co-parent, the shared cat and the care history that its remaining authorised carers need stay available to them, with your personal identity links cleaned.

If you own a cat that a co-parent also cares for, your request can enter a 14-day survival window. Shared household information is kept during that window and you can reclaim your account. Ownership is not transferred automatically. If the request expires, shared ownership needs to be resolved before a new request. We will explain any steps needed.

The 14-day window applies only to that shared-ownership situation. It is not a deadline for removing every copy or technical record. Under the current provider settings:

  • deleted photos can be recovered by the storage provider for up to 7 days;
  • deleted database data may remain in recovery copies for up to 7 days under managed point-in-time recovery; no scheduled database backups are configured;
  • ordinary technical logs are kept for 30 days, and required audit logs for 400 days, where such records exist;
  • Firebase Authentication keeps sign-in IP addresses for a few weeks, and completes deletion of a removed account, including from its backups, within 180 days.

Firebase installation identifiers follow their own lifecycle, and an OurCat account-deletion request does not currently delete them. They are replaced when the app is reinstalled or its data is cleared, and after 270 days of inactivity. Our encrypted local maintenance copies, made before launch, are retired seven days after each maintenance task is verified.

If a legal, security or other necessary exception requires us to keep something, we keep only what that purpose needs and explain the exception where we are allowed to. Deletion is a request and may not be immediate, but we will not delay verified requests unnecessarily.
