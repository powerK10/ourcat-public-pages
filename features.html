What it actually does

Here’s the app as it is at the moment, through a day of looking after a cat called Miso.

Request an invitation ↗How it works

It’s free. Android and iPhone/iPad invitations are available.

Afternoon

See it’s already been given

Sam gives Miso her supplement before work and logs it. In the afternoon you open Today, see it’s been given, and tap Like to say thanks, or hold Like to pick another emoji.

  1. Early evening

    What’s due today

    The water’s gone late. Reminders lists what’s due today, and whoever gets to it taps Done. Setting one up is quick: Common reminders has ready-made ones, like Clean litter tray. It’s a test version, so keep your usual reminders for medicine.

  2. When something looks off

    The vet’s number, ready to ring

    Care info is a cheat sheet for your cat, with the emergency vet’s and usual vet’s numbers. Tap one to ring. Health records, like vaccinations, are a tap away too. This is the bit I wished I’d had when Tokyo had an accident.

  3. Friday, going away

    A sitter, for as long as you choose

    In Care team, choose three days, a week or a month and send your friend the cat sitter code. They can log what they do and see the routine, while you stay in charge of the cat’s profile and reminders. Their access ends by itself, or sooner if you end it.

  4. Sharing is the bit I most want to see working. Try it with a partner or sitter.

    Request an invitation ↗

  5. Back home

    Who did what, and when

    History’s Day log shows who did what on any day, and Trends and Summary show how things have gone over time. If someone forgot to log something, you can add it later. If the vet asks, share a vet summary (PDF) or save a CSV of a date range.

  6. More than one cat

    Switch between your cats

    Tap the name at the top to switch between cats.

Made for shared care

OurCat is made for a cat that more than one person looks after, like a couple at home or an owner and their regular sitter, and it works on your own too. Everyone joins on their own phone or tablet, with their own invitation.

New to cats? The Features guide in Settings has tips for settling in a kitten, an adult cat or an adopted cat.

On an iPhone or iPad? Request an invitation and I’ll send you an invitation to TestFlight, Apple’s free app for trying early versions of apps.

Want to try it?

Send a short request and I’ll reply within a day. Partners and sitters send their own.
