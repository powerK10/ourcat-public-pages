What it actually does

Here’s the app as it is at the moment, through a day of looking after a cat called Miso.

Request an invitation ↗How it works

It’s free. Android and iPhone/iPad invitations are available.

Morning

Nobody gives the medicine twice

Sam gives Miso her joint supplement before work and logs it. Later you open Today, see it’s been given, and tap Like to say thanks, or hold Like to pick another emoji.

  1. Around lunchtime

    Nothing slips through

    The water’s gone late. Reminders lists what’s due today, and whoever gets to it taps Done. Setting one up is quick: Common reminders has ready-made ones, like Clean litter tray. It’s a test version, so keep your usual reminders for medicine.

  2. When something looks off

    The vet’s number, one tap away

    Care info is a cheat sheet for your cat, with the emergency vet’s and usual vet’s numbers. Tap one to ring. Health records, like vaccinations, are a tap away too. This is the bit I wished I’d had when Tokyo had an accident.

  3. Friday, going away

    A sitter, for as long as you choose

    In Care team, pick three days, a week or a month, and send your friend the cat sitter code. They can log what they do and read the routine in Care info, but can’t change the cat’s profile, the reminders or who’s on the care team. When the time’s up, their access ends by itself, or you can end it sooner.

  4. Sharing is the bit I most want to see working. Try it with a partner or sitter.

    Request an invitation ↗

  5. Back home

    Who did what, and when

    History’s Day log shows who did what on any day, and Trends and Summary show how things have gone over time. If someone forgot to log something, you can add it later (up to 30 days back). If the vet asks, share a vet summary (PDF) or save a CSV of a date range.

  6. More than one cat

    Up to three cats

    Tap the name at the top to switch between cats. A household can have up to three for now.

What it can’t do yet

It’s a test version. It keeps a record of care and that’s it. It won’t give vet advice or tell you if something is wrong. New to cats? The Features guide in Settings has optional tips for settling in a kitten, an adult cat or an adopted cat. A household can have up to three cats, shared by you, up to two other people and a cat sitter for a set time. Each person needs their own eligible device and invitation. It’s made for a cat that more than one person looks after, like a couple at home or an owner and their regular sitter.

Android phone alerts depend on your role, the test version and your notification settings. iPhone/iPad alerts are still being tested. Keep your usual reminders for medicine and anything urgent.

On an iPhone or iPad? Request an invitation and I’ll send you an invitation to TestFlight, Apple’s free app for trying early versions of apps.
